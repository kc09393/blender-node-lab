Goal

Create a material with Metallic ≥ 0.9, Roughness ≤ 0.2, connected to Material Output.

Progressive hints

  1. Hint 1：Which single slider most directly separates metal from plastic?
  2. Hint 2：Move Metallic close to 1, then decide whether the highlight is sharp enough.
  3. Hint 3：Reference: Metallic = 1 and Roughness around 0.12.

How to use this lab

Try the graph without revealing every hint. The interactive checker verifies the required node structure and values, then lets you compare your result with the reference graph.
