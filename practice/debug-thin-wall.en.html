Goal

Keep Thin Wall enabled and raise Transmission Weight to at least 0.8.

Progressive hints

  1. Hint 1：Thin Wall changes the transmission thickness model; it does not add transmission by itself.
  2. Hint 2：Raise Weight in Principled BSDF's Transmission section.

How to use this lab

Try the graph without revealing every hint. The interactive checker verifies the required node structure and values, then lets you compare your result with the reference graph.
