Channel Packing: Carry Three Masks in One Color

Pack three procedural masks into RGB and unpack them to drive different parameters.

Open the interactive activity 繁體中文

Goal

Pack masks with Combine Color, then unpack them into Roughness and Metallic.

Progressive hints

  1. Hint 1：Each R, G, and B channel can store an independent grayscale mask.
  2. Hint 2：Combine and Separate must be connected directly.

How to use this lab

Try the graph without revealing every hint. The interactive checker verifies the required node structure and values, then lets you compare your result with the reference graph.
