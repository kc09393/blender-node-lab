Make Brushed Metal

Drive Roughness with a Noise Texture to create metal with varying roughness — more realistic than a flat value.

Open the interactive tutorial 繁體中文
Intermediate4 steps

1. Step 1: Set Metallic to 1

On the Principled BSDF, push Metallic to maximum (1) so it reflects like metal instead of plastic.

2. Step 2: Add Texture Coordinate and Noise Texture

Drag in a Texture Coordinate (Input category) and a Noise Texture (Texture category), then connect Texture Coordinate's Generated output to Noise Texture's Vector input.

3. Step 3: Rescale with Map Range

Noise Texture's Fac output is 0-1 — too extreme for Roughness directly.

Add a Map Range (Converter category), feed Noise's Fac into it, and connect its output to Principled BSDF's Roughness.

4. Step 4: Confirm It Works

Check the live preview — the sphere's highlights should look uneven and brushed, not a perfectly uniform mirror.
