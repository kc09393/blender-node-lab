RGB Curves: Tone-Mapping Like Photoshop

The RGB Curves node lets you drag out a custom tone-mapping curve for a color — more flexible than Brightness/Contrast, letting you pull just the shadows or just the highlights. The exact same concept as Photoshop's Curves tool.

Open the interactive tutorial 繁體中文
Intermediate3 steps

1. Step 1: Add RGB Curves

Drag in RGB Curves from the Color category and connect it to Principled BSDF's Base Color.

2. Step 2: Drag the Midpoint Upward

Click the middle of the curve on the node card to add a control point, then drag it upward.

Midtones brighten while the darkest and brightest ends stay put — a common photo-editing move: 'lift the midtones'.

3. Step 3: Confirm the Effect

Check the live preview — the sphere should look brighter overall than the plain gray, without blowing out to solid white.

That's the power of curve-based 'local adjustment', more refined than a flat Brightness/Contrast slider.
