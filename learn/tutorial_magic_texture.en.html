Magic Texture: Abstract Pattern Generator

Magic Texture has no real-world material equivalent — it's a fun generator for abstract, kaleidoscope-like patterns, great for effects or stylized materials.

Open the interactive tutorial 繁體中文
Beginner4 steps

1. Step 1: Add a Magic Texture

Drag in a Magic Texture from the Texture category.

2. Step 2: Connect to Base Color

Connect its Color output to Principled BSDF's Base Color.

3. Step 3: Raise the Distortion

Raise Distortion above 3 — the pattern becomes more complex and kaleidoscope-like.

4. Step 4: Lower Roughness for a Glossy Look

Lower Principled BSDF's Roughness (e.g. below 0.3) so the pattern picks up some gloss — more like an effects material than a flat sticker.
