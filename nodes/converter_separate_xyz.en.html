Separate XYZ

Splits a vector into three separate values.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

When you want to operate on just one axis (e.g. scale only Y), use this node to split X/Y/Z apart first.

Technical notes

The inverse operation of Combine XYZ.
