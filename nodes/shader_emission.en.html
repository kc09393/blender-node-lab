Emission

Makes a surface emit light — useful for lamps, screens, neon.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Emission makes the material glow on its own, independent of scene lighting. Color sets the glow color, Strength sets brightness — higher values can blow out to white.

Technical notes

In real Cycles rendering, an Emission surface can light up other objects (it becomes a light source). This live preview only shows it as self-emissive; it won't illuminate other surfaces in the scene.
