Glossy BSDF

A purely reflective, mirror-like surface — polished metal, mirrors.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Glossy BSDF only models reflection — no scattered base color like Diffuse BSDF. Roughness controls how sharp the reflection is (0 = mirror-clear, 1 = blurry/frosted). Often mixed with Diffuse BSDF via Mix Shader to create a 'mostly plastic, glossy at the edges' car-paint look.

Technical notes

This sandbox implements Glossy BSDF as metallic=1 (pure reflection, base color tints the reflection). This isn't identical to Blender's actual GGX microfacet model, but is enough to teach the 'pure reflection vs. pure diffuse' distinction.
