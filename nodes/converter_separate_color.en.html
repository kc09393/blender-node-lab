Separate Color

Splits a color into three (or four) separate values, in the chosen color space.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

When you want to process a single channel (e.g. boost only red), split it apart with this node, adjust, and rejoin with Combine Color. Choosing HSV/HSL instead splits into hue/saturation/value(or lightness) — handy when you want to adjust brightness or saturation without touching hue.

Technical notes

Mode has 3 options matching Blender: RGB, HSV, HSL. Switching mode changes what the first three outputs mean (R/G/B ↔ H/S/V ↔ H/S/L); the RGB-to-HSV/HSL formulas are the same ones used by Color Ramp (`js/core/colorRampUtil.js`), cross-checked for consistency.
