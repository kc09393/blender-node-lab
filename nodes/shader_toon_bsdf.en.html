Toon BSDF

Splits lighting into hard bands for a cartoon/cel-shaded look.

繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Toon BSDF makes the light/shadow boundary sharp (stepped, not smooth gradient) — common for anime-style rendering.

Technical notes

A true stepped look requires quantizing *after* the lighting calculation, not before — different from this sandbox's current 'hand off material params, let Three.js light it' architecture. Planned for a future upgrade.
