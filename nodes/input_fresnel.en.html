Fresnel

Computes how much a surface faces the camera vs. grazes it — used for stronger edge reflections.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Almost every real material reflects less when viewed head-on and more at grazing angles — the Fresnel effect (think of looking straight down into a pool vs. across its surface). Wiring Fresnel into a Mix Shader's Fac creates this edge-brightening look.

Technical notes

The IOR socket controls how strong the edge effect is (higher = more pronounced). This sandbox computes it from the view direction and the geometric (pre-Bump) normal — a simplification; ideally it should use the final normal after Bump is applied.
