Brightness/Contrast

Brightens/darkens overall, and stretches or compresses the light-dark range.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Bright directly adds/subtracts brightness, Contrast stretches (or compresses) the gap between light and dark areas. Same concept as the brightness/contrast sliders in a phone's photo editor.

Technical notes

The formula mirrors Blender's: Contrast scales the color relative to mid-gray (0.5), then Bright is added, and the result is clamped to 0-1.
