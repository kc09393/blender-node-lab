Geometry

Provides surface position, normal, tangent, and other geometric data.

繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Position and Normal are the two most common outputs, similar in effect to Texture Coordinate's Object/Normal.

Technical notes

Advanced outputs like Pointiness (edge/crevice detection) and Random Per Island need mesh topology analysis unavailable in this live-preview pipeline. The whole node is documented for reference; Position/Normal are available via the Texture Coordinate node instead.
