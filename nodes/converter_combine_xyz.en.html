Combine XYZ

Combines three separate values into a single vector.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

When you've computed X, Y, Z separately (e.g. via Math nodes) and need to combine them into one coordinate/direction, this node joins them.

Technical notes

The inverse operation of Separate XYZ.
