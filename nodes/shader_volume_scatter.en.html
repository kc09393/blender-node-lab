Volume Scatter

Scatters light within a volume — even smoke or cloud/fog effects.

繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Often used with Volume Absorption: Absorption darkens light, Scatter softens/spreads it.

Technical notes

Also requires ray marching to render correctly — not yet supported in live preview.
