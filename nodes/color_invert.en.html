Invert Color

Flips a color to its complement — like a photo negative.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Invert Color computes 1 minus the original value (white becomes black, red becomes cyan). Fac controls how much inversion is applied: 0 = no change, 1 = fully inverted.

Technical notes

Commonly used to flip a black-and-white mask (e.g. turning 'where there's noise' into 'where there isn't'), or to quickly get a complementary color scheme.
