Volume Absorption

Absorbs light within a volume — colored smoke or foggy glass interiors.

繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Connects to Material Output's Volume socket (not Surface) to give an object's interior a light-absorbing effect.

Technical notes

Volume rendering requires multi-step ray marching through the interior. This preview only handles Surface — Volume is documented but not yet supported.
