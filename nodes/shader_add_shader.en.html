Add Shader

Adds two shaders' light contribution together (not averaged).

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Similar to Mix Shader but without a Fac ratio — both inputs are simply added together. Often used to layer extra reflection or glow on top of a base material.

Technical notes

Approximation used here: base parameters are averaged, emission is summed, and the stronger Transmission, Coat, and Thin Film layers are kept to avoid invalid ranges. This is not true radiance addition, but it demonstrates the layering concept.
