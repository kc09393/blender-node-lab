Diffuse BSDF

A purely rough, non-reflective surface like chalk or paper.

Try it in the sandbox 繁體中文

Beginner explanation

Diffuse BSDF only models light scattering evenly in all directions — no metal or transmission options. Good for fully matte materials, or for learning the simplest possible shader node.

Technical notes

Blender's Diffuse BSDF uses Oren-Nayar (not pure Lambert) when Roughness > 0. This preview folds Roughness into the final material roughness rather than doing a per-ray Oren-Nayar integral.
